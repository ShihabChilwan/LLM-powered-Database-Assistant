# Customer Service FAQ

Larkspur Provisions, Customer Service
Version 3.2, effective 1 July 2016

Customer Service is open Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 Pacific Time, on +1 206 555 0142 or at **service@larkspur-provisions.example**. We answer emails within **1 business day**.

The answers below summarise our policies. If an answer and a policy ever differ, the policy applies.

## Orders and delivery

### How quickly will my order be dispatched?

We aim to dispatch within 10 calendar days of the order date, and contact you if dispatch will take more than 21 days. Delivery times are then counted in business days from the shipped date. See the Shipping and Delivery Policy, section 2.

### Which shipper is fastest?

Speedy Express. It delivers within 2 business days in the USA and Canada, and within 4 business days to Europe. United Package is our standard service and Federal Shipping is best for large orders of 20 cases or more. See the Shipping and Delivery Policy, section 4.

### My delivery is late. Am I entitled to compensation?

Yes, if it arrived more than 2 business days after the guaranteed maximum for your shipper and zone. You receive a credit note of 5% of the goods value (3 to 5 days late) or 10% (more than 5 days late), up to $500 per order. Claim within 14 days of delivery. See the Shipping and Delivery Policy, section 5.

### Who pays import duties on international orders?

You do. We ship on DAP terms: we pay the freight, and the customer pays import duties, taxes and brokerage. See the Shipping and Delivery Policy, section 6.

## Damaged goods and returns

### My order arrived damaged. What should I do?

Report it with the order ID, the products and quantities affected, and at least two photos. The deadline is 48 hours after delivery for perishable goods (Dairy Products, Meat/Poultry, Seafood, Produce) and 7 calendar days for all other goods. You can choose a replacement, a full refund or a credit note. See the Returns and Refunds Policy, section 3.

### My cheese arrived warm. Does that count as damaged?

Yes, if chilled goods arrive above 8 °C, or frozen goods warmer than −12 °C. Please measure and report the temperature. See the Product Storage and Handling Policy, section 5.

### Can I return goods I no longer need?

Only non-perishable goods, unopened, within 30 calendar days of delivery. You receive a credit note minus a 15% restocking fee, and you pay the return freight. Perishable goods cannot be returned for change of mind. See the Returns and Refunds Policy, section 4.

### How long does a refund take?

Approved refunds are paid within 10 business days of approval. Credit notes are valid for 12 months. See the Returns and Refunds Policy, section 5.

## Payment and invoices

### When do I have to pay?

Within 30 calendar days of the invoice date (net 30). If you pay within 10 days, you may deduct 2%. Overdue amounts are charged 1.5% per month. See the Payment and Invoicing Policy, sections 2 and 3.

### Do you offer discounts on large orders?

Yes. Orders of $2,000 or more earn a 3% rebate, $5,000 or more earn 5%, and $10,000 or more earn 7%. The rebate arrives as a credit note on your monthly statement. See the Payment and Invoicing Policy, section 6.

### I think my invoice is wrong. How do I dispute it?

Write to billing within 15 calendar days of the invoice date, quoting the order ID. Pay the undisputed part on time; no interest is charged on the disputed amount while we check it. See the Payment and Invoicing Policy, section 4.

## Products

### How fresh will the products be?

Every product leaves our warehouse with a minimum remaining shelf life, for example 21 days for Dairy Products and 90 days for Beverages. See the Product Storage and Handling Policy, section 4.
