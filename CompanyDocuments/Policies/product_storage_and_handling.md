# Product Storage and Handling Policy

Larkspur Provisions, Warehouse Operations and Quality
Policy version 3.2, effective 1 July 2016

## 1. Scope

This policy sets the storage conditions for each of our eight product categories, the minimum shelf life a product must have when it is dispatched to a customer, and what counts as damaged goods. It applies in our Seattle warehouse and is also our recommendation to customers for storing goods after delivery.

## 2. General rules

- Goods are stored on pallets or shelving at least **15 cm above the floor** and **45 cm away from walls**.
- Stock is picked **first expired, first out (FEFO)**: the unit with the earliest best-before date leaves first.
- Strong-smelling goods (cheeses, cured fish, spices) are stored apart from confectionery and grains, which absorb odours.
- Temperature in every chilled and frozen room is logged automatically **every 15 minutes**. An alarm is raised if a room is outside its range for more than **30 minutes**.

## 3. Storage conditions by category

| Category | Storage type | Temperature | Other conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beverages | Ambient | 10 to 20 °C | Out of direct light; wine bottles stored lying down |
| Condiments | Ambient | 10 to 25 °C | Dry; opened containers are never returned to stock |
| Confections | Cool | 12 to 18 °C | Humidity below 55%; chocolate kept away from odours |
| Dairy Products | Chilled | 2 to 5 °C | Soft cheeses (Mascarpone, Mozzarella) at 2 to 4 °C |
| Grains/Cereals | Ambient | 10 to 25 °C | Humidity below 60%; pest checks every week |
| Meat/Poultry | Frozen | −18 °C or colder | Never refrozen once thawed |
| Produce | Chilled | 1 to 5 °C | Tofu products and dried fruit kept in sealed packs |
| Seafood | Frozen or chilled | Frozen −18 °C or colder; chilled 0 to 3 °C | Cured and smoked fish chilled; all other seafood frozen |

Dairy Products, Meat/Poultry, Seafood and Produce are our **perishable** categories. This grouping is also used in the Returns and Refunds Policy.

## 4. Shelf-life rules

### 4.1 Minimum remaining shelf life at dispatch

A product may only be dispatched to a customer if it has at least this much time left before its best-before or use-by date:

| Category | Minimum remaining shelf life at dispatch |
|---|---|
| Beverages | 90 days |
| Condiments | 120 days |
| Confections | 90 days |
| Dairy Products | 21 days |
| Grains/Cereals | 120 days |
| Meat/Poultry | 60 days |
| Produce | 14 days |
| Seafood | 30 days |

### 4.2 Short-dated stock

Stock that falls below its dispatch minimum is moved to a separate **short-dated** area. It can be sold to customers only with their written agreement, at a discount of at least **30%**, and it cannot be returned for any reason except damage in transit.

### 4.3 Expired stock

Expired stock is never sold. It is recorded and disposed of within **48 hours** of the expiry date.

## 5. What counts as damaged

Goods are **damaged** if, on delivery, any of the following is true:

1. The inner packaging is torn, punctured, crushed or leaking, or a seal is broken.
2. Cans are dented on a seam, swollen or rusted.
3. Glass bottles or jars are cracked or broken.
4. **Chilled goods** arrive above **8 °C**, or **frozen goods** arrive warmer than **−12 °C** (cold-chain breach).
5. The product is past its best-before date, or has less remaining shelf life than the dispatch minimum in section 4.1 (unless it was sold as short-dated).
6. There are signs of pests, mould or water damage.

These are **not** damage:

- Scuffs, marks or dents on an outer carton when the inner packs are intact.
- Normal settling of contents (for example in bags of grains).
- Frost on the outside of frozen packs that are still at −12 °C or colder.

## 6. Handling on receipt (customers)

We recommend that customers move chilled and frozen goods into storage within **30 minutes** of delivery, check the temperature of one pack per box, and photograph any damage before unpacking further. These photos are required for claims (see the Returns and Refunds Policy, section 3.2).
