# Payment and Invoicing Policy

Larkspur Provisions, Finance Department
Policy version 3.2, effective 1 July 2016

## 1. Invoices

- An invoice is issued for every order **on the order date**. The invoice number is the order ID.
- The invoice lists each product with its quantity and unit price, and the total price of the goods.
- Freight is not on the order invoice; it is billed on a separate monthly freight statement (see the Shipping and Delivery Policy, section 7).
- All prices and invoices are in **US dollars (USD)**.

## 2. Payment terms

### 2.1 Standard terms

Our standard terms are **net 30**: payment is due **30 calendar days after the invoice date**.

### 2.2 Early-payment discount

Customers who pay the full invoice within **10 calendar days** of the invoice date may deduct **2%** of the goods value ("2/10 net 30"). The discount does not apply to freight statements.

### 2.3 New accounts

A new customer's **first 3 orders** are paid in advance by bank transfer. After that, the account moves to standard net 30 terms with a credit limit of **$10,000**. Customers can ask for a higher limit after 6 months without late payments.

### 2.4 Payment methods

- Bank transfer (preferred), quoting the order ID as reference.
- Credit card, for invoices up to **$5,000**.
- Credit notes, which can be applied to any open invoice (see section 5).

## 3. Late payment

### 3.1 Late-payment fees

- Overdue amounts are charged interest of **1.5% per month**, calculated daily, starting on the day after the due date.
- A reminder is sent **7 days** after the due date, and a second reminder at **21 days**.

### 3.2 Credit hold and collections

- An account with any invoice more than **30 days overdue** is placed on **credit hold**: new orders are accepted only with payment in advance.
- Invoices more than **90 days overdue** are passed to an external collections agency, and collection costs are added to the balance.
- Credit hold is lifted as soon as all overdue invoices and fees are paid.

## 4. Invoice disputes

1. Disputes must be raised **within 15 calendar days** of the invoice date, in writing to **billing@larkspur-provisions.example**, quoting the order ID and the lines disputed.
2. The undisputed part of the invoice must still be paid by the due date.
3. No late-payment interest is charged on the disputed amount while the dispute is open.
4. Finance replies within **10 business days**. If the dispute is upheld, a corrected invoice or a credit note is issued.
5. Problems with the goods themselves (damage, wrong products) are handled as claims under the Returns and Refunds Policy, not as invoice disputes.

## 5. Credit notes and refunds

- Credit notes are valid for **12 months** and can be used against any invoice.
- Refunds for approved claims are paid within **10 business days** of approval (see the Returns and Refunds Policy, section 5).
- If the customer has overdue invoices, refunds are first set off against the overdue amount.

## 6. Volume discounts for large orders

Large orders earn a volume rebate based on the **goods value of a single order** (the total price on the invoice):

| Order goods value | Rebate |
|---|---|
| Below $2,000 | None |
| $2,000 to $4,999.99 | 3% |
| $5,000 to $9,999.99 | 5% |
| $10,000 or more | 7% |

Rules:

- The rebate is **not deducted from the invoice**. The invoice shows the full total price, and the rebate is issued as a credit note on the monthly statement, within **15 days** of the end of the month in which the order was paid.
- Rebates are earned only on invoices paid on time (within 30 days).
- Separate orders cannot be combined to reach a higher tier.
- The volume rebate and the 2% early-payment discount can both apply to the same order.

## 7. Contact

Finance questions: **billing@larkspur-provisions.example**, Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00 Pacific Time.
