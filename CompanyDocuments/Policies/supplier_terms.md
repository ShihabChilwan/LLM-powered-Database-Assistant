# Supplier Terms and Purchasing Rules

Larkspur Provisions, Purchasing Department
Policy version 3.2, effective 1 July 2016

## 1. Scope

This document sets out when we reorder stock, how much we order, the lead times we agree with our suppliers, and the quality checks we carry out when goods arrive. It applies to all suppliers of the 77 products in our range.

## 2. Monthly stock review

Purchasing reviews stock once a month, using the **monthly stock report for each category**. For each product the report shows the units sold in that month, the units in stock at the end of the month, and the unit price.

Units in stock is a snapshot at the end of the month. Reorder decisions are always based on the **latest month's** units in stock, never on a total across months.

## 3. Reorder rules

### 3.1 Reorder level

A product is reordered when its units in stock in the latest monthly report fall **below its reorder level**:

| Category | Reorder level (units in stock) |
|---|---|
| Beverages, Condiments, Confections, Grains/Cereals | below **20** units |
| Dairy Products, Meat/Poultry, Seafood, Produce | below **10** units |

Perishable categories have a lower reorder level because holding large stocks of them increases waste.

### 3.2 Reorder quantity

The reorder quantity is enough for **two months of sales**: twice the average monthly units sold over the **last 3 monthly reports**, minus the current units in stock, rounded up to a full case. The minimum order is one case.

### 3.3 Out-of-stock products

A product with **0 units in stock** is out of stock. Purchasing places an **urgent order on the same business day** and informs Customer Service, who tell customers with open orders for that product.

### 3.4 Discontinued products

A product is reviewed for removal from the range when it sells **fewer than 10 units in total over 3 consecutive months**. Discontinued products are not reordered.

## 4. Supplier lead times

The agreed lead time is the number of calendar days from our purchase order to delivery at our Seattle warehouse:

| Category | Standard lead time |
|---|---|
| Beverages | 14 days |
| Condiments | 21 days |
| Confections | 14 days |
| Dairy Products | 10 days |
| Grains/Cereals | 21 days |
| Meat/Poultry | 10 days |
| Produce | 7 days |
| Seafood | 10 days |

Products made outside North America have an extra **14 days** of lead time for sea freight and customs.

Suppliers must confirm each purchase order within **2 business days** and warn us immediately about any delay of more than **3 days**.

## 5. Quality checks on arrival

### 5.1 Timing

- Chilled and frozen deliveries are checked within **2 hours** of arrival.
- All other deliveries are checked within **24 hours** of arrival.

### 5.2 What we check

1. **Temperature:** chilled goods must be at or below **5 °C** and frozen goods at **−18 °C** or colder on arrival. The temperature of one case per pallet is recorded.
2. **Sampling:** we open **1 case in every 10**, and at least 2 cases per delivery, and inspect the inner packs against the damage list in the Product Storage and Handling Policy, section 5.
3. **Shelf life:** goods must arrive with at least **twice** the minimum remaining shelf life at dispatch set in the Product Storage and Handling Policy, section 4.1. For example, Dairy Products must arrive with at least 42 days left, and Beverages with at least 180 days.
4. **Paperwork:** the delivery note must match the purchase order on product, quantity and batch numbers.

### 5.3 Rejected deliveries

- Goods that fail any check are rejected and held apart for collection.
- The supplier collects rejected goods at its own cost within **5 business days** and issues a credit within **30 days**.
- If more than **20%** of the sampled cases fail, the whole delivery is rejected.

## 6. Supplier payment and performance

- Suppliers are paid on **net 45** terms from the date the goods pass the arrival checks.
- We expect at least **95%** of deliveries to arrive on time and in full.
- A supplier with **3 or more rejected deliveries in 6 months** is put on review. During the review, Purchasing may move orders to another supplier.
