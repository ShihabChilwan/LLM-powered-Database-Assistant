# Shipping and Delivery Policy

Larkspur Provisions, Logistics Department
Policy version 3.2, effective 1 July 2016

## 1. Scope

This policy describes our three shipping partners, the delivery times we commit to, what happens when a delivery is late, and how international shipments work. All orders are dispatched from our central warehouse in Seattle, Washington, USA.

## 2. Dispatch

We aim to dispatch orders within **10 calendar days** of the order date. Busy periods and stock shortages can make dispatch take longer; when an order will be dispatched more than 21 days after the order date, Customer Service contacts the customer.

The **shipped date** on the shipping order is the day the goods leave our warehouse. **All delivery times in this policy are counted in business days from the shipped date**, not from the order date. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in the destination country.

## 3. Delivery zones

Zones follow the shipping regions printed on our shipping orders:

| Zone | Regions | Countries we ship to |
|---|---|---|
| Zone A | North America | USA, Canada |
| Zone B | Central America | Mexico |
| Zone C | Europe: British Isles, Western, Southern, Northern and Eastern Europe, Scandinavia | UK, Ireland, France, Germany, Belgium, Switzerland, Austria, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Norway, Poland |
| Zone D | South America | Brazil, Venezuela, Argentina |

## 4. Our shippers

We work with three shippers. The shipper ID appears on every shipping order.

### 4.1 Speedy Express (shipper ID 1)

Express air service for urgent orders and for perishable goods that must arrive quickly. Chilled and frozen goods are packed in insulated boxes with gel packs or dry ice, rated for 72 hours.

### 4.2 United Package (shipper ID 2)

Our standard service and the default for most orders. Economical for small and medium orders of non-perishable goods. Chilled and frozen goods sent to Zones C and D with United Package are packed with extra refrigerant rated for 120 hours.

### 4.3 Federal Shipping (shipper ID 3)

Consolidated freight with temperature-controlled containers. Recommended for large orders of 20 cases or more, and for full pallets.

### 4.4 Delivery times

Typical delivery time and **guaranteed maximum**, in business days from the shipped date:

| Shipper | Zone A | Zone B | Zone C | Zone D |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Speedy Express | 1–2 (max 2) | 2–3 (max 3) | 3–4 (max 4) | 3–5 (max 5) |
| United Package | 3–5 (max 5) | 5–7 (max 7) | 7–10 (max 10) | 8–12 (max 12) |
| Federal Shipping | 4–6 (max 6) | 6–8 (max 8) | 6–9 (max 9) | 9–14 (max 14) |

For perishable goods (Dairy Products, Meat/Poultry, Seafood and Produce) we recommend Speedy Express for every zone, or Federal Shipping for large orders.

## 5. Late-delivery compensation

A delivery is **late** when it arrives after the guaranteed maximum for its shipper and zone (section 4.4).

| How late | Compensation |
|---|---|
| 1–2 business days after the maximum | None (within tolerance) |
| 3–5 business days after the maximum | Credit note for **5%** of the order's goods value |
| More than 5 business days after the maximum | Credit note for **10%** of the order's goods value |

Rules:

- Compensation is always a **credit note**, never cash, and is capped at **$500 per order**.
- Claims must be made within **14 calendar days** of delivery, quoting the order ID.
- Delays caused by customs inspections, an incorrect delivery address, or the customer being unable to receive the goods do not count.
- If perishable goods arrive late **and** spoiled, the customer can also claim for the spoiled goods under the Returns and Refunds Policy. Both claims can be made for the same order.

## 6. International shipping

Orders to Zones B, C and D are international shipments.

- We ship on **DAP (Delivered at Place)** terms: we pay freight to the destination, and the **customer pays import duties, taxes and customs brokerage fees**.
- Every international shipment travels with a commercial invoice. Dairy Products, Meat/Poultry and Seafood also travel with a health certificate, which adds **2 business days** to dispatch.
- Days spent in customs inspection do not count towards the delivery times in section 4.4.
- If goods are refused at the border because of missing import permits on the customer's side, the return freight is charged to the customer.

## 7. Freight charges

Freight is charged on a separate monthly freight statement, not on the order invoice. Orders with a goods value of **$500 or more** shipped with United Package within Zone A travel free of charge.
