# Returns and Refunds Policy

Larkspur Provisions, Customer Operations
Policy version 3.2, effective 1 July 2016

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains when and how trade customers can return goods, report damaged or incorrect deliveries, and receive a refund or a credit note. It applies to every order placed with Larkspur Provisions, whichever shipper delivered it (Speedy Express, United Package or Federal Shipping).

Delivery times and compensation for late deliveries are covered in the Shipping and Delivery Policy. Storage temperatures and the full definition of "damaged" are in the Product Storage and Handling Policy. Payment of refunds against open invoices is covered in the Payment and Invoicing Policy.

## 2. Perishable and non-perishable goods

Every product belongs to one of our eight product categories. For returns, the categories are split into two groups:

- **Perishable:** Dairy Products, Meat/Poultry, Seafood and Produce.
- **Non-perishable:** Beverages, Condiments, Confections and Grains/Cereals.

A product is treated according to its category, whatever its packaging. For example, canned seafood is still handled as Seafood (perishable), and Longlife Tofu is still handled as Produce (perishable).

## 3. Damaged, wrong or missing goods

### 3.1 Reporting deadlines

Problems must be reported within these deadlines, counted from the date of delivery:

| Product group | Damaged or spoiled goods | Wrong product or wrong quantity |
|---|---|---|
| Perishable | 48 hours | 48 hours |
| Non-perishable | 7 calendar days | 7 calendar days |

Claims reported after the deadline are declined, except where the damage could not reasonably have been seen on delivery (for example, a sealed inner pack that is found to be spoiled when opened). In that case the claim must be made within 48 hours of opening and before the best-before date.

If one order contains both perishable and non-perishable products, each product line follows the deadline of its own group.

### 3.2 What we need from you

Each claim must include:

1. The order ID (shown on the invoice and the shipping order).
2. The product name and the quantity affected.
3. At least two photos: one of the damaged goods and one of the outer packaging or shipping label.
4. For chilled or frozen goods, the product temperature measured on arrival, if available.

### 3.3 What we offer

For damaged, spoiled, wrong or short-shipped goods, the customer chooses one of:

- **A replacement**, shipped at our cost with the same shipper and service level as the original order.
- **A full refund** of the affected lines (unit price × quantity affected, as shown on the invoice).
- **A credit note** for the same amount.

Perishable goods do not need to be sent back. Once the claim is approved, the customer disposes of them locally and keeps the photos as evidence. Damaged non-perishable goods are collected by us at our cost, unless the claim is below $50, in which case the customer may dispose of them.

## 4. Returns of goods in good condition (change of mind)

- **Perishable goods cannot be returned** for change of mind.
- **Non-perishable goods** can be returned within **30 calendar days** of delivery if they are unopened, in their original packaging, and have at least 90 days left before their best-before date.
- Change-of-mind returns receive a **credit note only** (no cash refund), minus a **15% restocking fee**.
- The customer pays the return freight.

## 5. Refund or credit note: how each works

### 5.1 Refunds

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within **10 business days** of approval. If the order has not been paid yet, the refund is instead deducted from the open invoice.

### 5.2 Credit notes

A credit note is valid for **12 months** from its issue date. It can be used against any future invoice, in full or in part, and has no cash value. Credit notes from returns, from late-delivery compensation and from volume rebates can be combined on one invoice.

## 6. How to report a problem

1. Open a claim in the customer portal, or email **returns@larkspur-provisions.example**, or call Customer Service on +1 206 555 0142 (Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 Pacific Time).
2. We issue a **Return Authorisation (RA) number** within **2 business days**. Goods sent back without an RA number are refused.
3. We decide on the claim within **5 business days** of receiving complete information.
4. If the decision is disputed, the customer can ask for a review by the Customer Operations Manager within 14 days of the decision.

## 7. Exclusions

We do not accept claims for:

- Goods damaged after delivery by incorrect storage at the customer's premises.
- Goods refused or held up because the delivery address was wrong or nobody was available to receive them.
- Minor marks on outer cartons where the inner packs are intact (see the Product Storage and Handling Policy, section 5).
- Claims with a total value below **$10**.
